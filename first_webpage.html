Info on Halloween

The history of Halloween

The oldest record we have of this holiday came from a tribe called the Celts. They originally made it as a celebration of the eve of the new year. Their calender would restart at October. This was said to be the day the the land of the dead and the land of the living mixed and the differences between them became blurred. The reason to why they thought that this was whn the dead came back to life was because after October it would get very cold and people would get sick and die. Their crops also died around this time. To celebrate they would Wear animal skins and predict ones future around large camp fires.

Halloween Nowdays

Nowdays during Halloween instead of dressing in animal skins we wear costumes, and instead of telling fortunes we tell scary stories. Kids will Trick-or-Treat and go to others doors, knock, and get candy. Sometimes if ther person doesn't give candy childeren will throw eggs at their house. All very fun.
