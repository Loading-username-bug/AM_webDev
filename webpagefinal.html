Dead Horse Point

All About

This National Park is a definitly worth the trip. While you are there you will see 2,000ft worth of sheer cliff that reveal red rocks, winding rivers, magnificent veiws of sunsets and a lifes worth of star gazing.

Halloween Nowadays

Nowadays during Halloween instead of dressing in animal skins we wear costumes, and instead of telling fortunes we tell scary stories. Kids will Trick-or-Treat and go to others doors, knock, and get candy. Sometimes if ther person doesn't give candy childeren will throw eggs at their house. All very fun.

Common activities include:

  • Hiking trails leading to over 8 canyon viewpoints
  • Mountain Biking to new destinations
  • Campgrounds with Sightseeing and stargazing
  • Picnic pavilions and dog friendly walks
